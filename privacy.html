Xpress Brew

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026 · Version 1.0

Xpress Brew is published by Castor & Pollux, Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Short version: we collect only what the app needs, we never sell your data, and you can delete your account in the app at any time.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and region. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email they share (Apple lets you hide your email).
  • Your coffee data: recipes, brew history and taste feedback, reviews, Circles you create or join, and taste profiles you add.
  • Bag photos: when you scan a coffee bag, the photo is sent to our AI provider to read the label. We don't store the photo.
  • Business applications: business name, website, country, licence number and links you submit for a verified badge.
  • Purchases: Apple or Google process payments. We receive your subscription status, never your card details.

What stays on your phone: the Bluetooth connection to your coffee machine and its live readings (scale, water, status). Camera images used for QR codes are read on the device.

We do not use advertising trackers, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or rent personal data.

Why we use it

  • To run your account, save and sync your recipes, and show your reviews.
  • To make recipes from bag photos and adjust them from your taste feedback.
  • To apply your plan (for example the monthly scan limit) and prevent abuse.
  • To review business applications and moderate reported reviews.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run the app, under contracts that limit their use of your data:

  • Supabase — accounts and database.
  • Anthropic — reads coffee-bag photos (AI).
  • Apple and Google — subscriptions and payments (we never see your card details).
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website.

What others can see: your name, ratings and review text are public to other users. Recipes are private unless you share them with a Circle or publish them as a verified business. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account (Me → Account → Delete my account), we delete your profile, recipes, reviews, brew history and Circles you own. Backups are cleared within 30 days.

Your rights

You can see and edit most of your data in the app. You can also ask us for a copy, a correction or deletion, or object to how we use it, through our contact form (or Me → Help & support in the app). We follow the UAE Personal Data Protection Law and, for users in the EU/UK, the GDPR.

Security and location

Data is encrypted in transit and protected by access rules so people can only see what they're allowed to. Our providers may store data outside the UAE; we use providers with recognised safeguards.

Children

Xpress Brew is not meant for children under 13 (under 16 in the EU). We don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
